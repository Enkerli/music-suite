Twirl

Twirl guide ↗ · Source ↗

Twirl now lives on GitHub Pages, with the rest of the Music Suite: enkerli.github.io/music-suite/apps/twirl. Codes made here open there too.

Tap “Keep a loop”, then tap any loop on the sheet: it becomes a toy you can drop again, or share. Stored in this browser only.

Empty slot: tap to save. Full slot: tap to recall; hold it (or Shift + tap, Shift + Enter) to overwrite. Stored in this browser only.

Paste the whole thing, even if it came through broken across lines. A sheet replaces yours (yours is kept: Back to my sheet); toys join My toys; “everything” fills your empty scene slots and adds its toys.

Press ● Rec (or Shift+Space) and play; press it again to stop. A take keeps what you did (strokes, moves, settings): Replay it here, even after a reload, or send it as a code. Its sound stays until you close the page: save the ones you want to keep.

Keyboard

With a keyboard, you can steer without touching the sheet, where every touch draws. Everything else is in the Twirl guide ↗.

On iPad: tap the page once so it hears the keyboard. No Esc key? ` (top left) or ⌘ . does the same.

Twirl now lives on GitHub Pages, with the rest of the Music Suite: enkerli.github.io/music-suite/apps/twirl. Codes made here open there too.

Tap “Keep a loop”, then tap any loop on the sheet: it becomes a toy you can drop again, or share. Stored in this browser only.

Empty slot: tap to save. Full slot: tap to recall; hold it (or Shift + tap, Shift + Enter) to overwrite. Stored in this browser only.

Paste the whole thing, even if it came through broken across lines. A sheet replaces yours (yours is kept: Back to my sheet); toys join My toys; “everything” fills your empty scene slots and adds its toys.

Press ● Rec (or Shift+Space) and play; press it again to stop. A take keeps what you did (strokes, moves, settings): Replay it here, even after a reload, or send it as a code. Its sound stays until you close the page: save the ones you want to keep.

Keyboard

With a keyboard, you can steer without touching the sheet, where every touch draws. Everything else is in the Twirl guide ↗.

On iPad: tap the page once so it hears the keyboard. No Esc key? ` (top left) or ⌘ . does the same.

Space
Play / pause, wherever the focus is, except inside an open panel (there it presses the focused button). Enter presses a focused button
⇧ Space
Record / stop (takes are in Toybox → Takes)
1…9
Select a loop (its number is on the sheet and in the chips). Tab ⇧ Tab step through the loops. 0 or Esc deselects
Enter
The selected loop's settings (Enter again closes them)
←↑↓→ , .
Move the selected loop (⇧: further) · turn it
< > X D
Selected loop slower / faster · mute it · duplicate it
⇧1…⇧6
Pen: direction · drums · pluck · bell · blip · shaker
V
Show or hide the pen palette
⌫ or ⌘Z
Erase the selected loop, or the newest one (undo)
⇧⌘Z or Ctrl Y
Bring it back (redo)
⇧⌫
Clear the sheet (press twice)
L M E
Lock · Morph · Edit notes
P N B G
Next pen · notes density · sound pack · grid. With ⇧: previous
[ ]
Tempo down / up. With ⇧: by 10
- =
Volume down / up
T Y S
Toybox · My toys · Scenes
C
Controls drawer (from there, Tab moves through the controls)
F
Full screen, on and off
1…9, 10…14
Pick a loop (two digits quickly for 10–14); in its settings, PageDown / PageUp (fn ↓ / fn ↑) for the next or previous loop, and Tab past the last control moves on to the next loop
I
Messages: shown as they happen, or quiet behind the dot
Q ⇧Q
Diagnostics: frames a second, how late the clock runs, notes a second, errors, where the last tap landed · ⇧Q copies a report
\
Simple ↔ Everything
R W O
Revoice (new loops) · Walk: off, then each way in turn (⇧W back) · Drone
Esc `
Close whatever is open
H ?
This list (? where it isn't a shifted digit; ⇧ digits always pick pens)

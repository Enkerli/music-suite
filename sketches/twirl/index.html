Controls

Twirl On: turning a loop changes its voices. Off: voices stay as drawn. Every two bars, everything shifts along a little progression. A soft root and fifth underneath, following Walk.

Tap “Keep a loop”, then tap any loop on the sheet: it becomes a toy you can drop again, or share. Stored in this browser only.

Empty slot: tap to save. Full slot: tap to recall, hold to overwrite. Stored in this browser only.

Every stroke plays as you draw it, then keeps looping on its own clock. Spirals climb and tumble through the scale. Scribble fast back and forth for a shaker. Drag a loop to move it; with two fingers, turn it or resize it: bigger plays slower. Turn on Lock and notes fire only where a line is crossed.
